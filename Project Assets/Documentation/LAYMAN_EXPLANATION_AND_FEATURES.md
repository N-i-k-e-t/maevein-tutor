# Maevein Tutor — Plain English & Layman's Feature Guide

> **Everything You Need to Know About Maevein Tutor Explained Simply — Purpose, Who It Is For, and Key Features.**

---

## 💡 1. What is the Purpose of Maevein Tutor? (In Simple Words)

Imagine having a **personal 1-on-1 private tutor available 24/7** sitting right inside your phone or computer.

### The Problem Today
When students take regular online quizzes or tests in school, they usually just get a score like `65%` or `7/10`.
- They don't know **why** they lost marks.
- They don't know **which exact topic from the textbook** they misunderstood.
- The computer just tells them if they are "Right" or "Wrong" based on exact spelling, penalizing students who actually understand the concept but explain it in their own words.

### How Maevein Tutor Fixes This
**Maevein Tutor** acts as an **intelligent AI teacher**. You give it any textbook chapter or syllabus PDF, and it:
1. Creates a custom practice test specifically from your reading material.
2. Reads your written answers like a human teacher and understands your meaning (even if you spell things differently).
3. Shows you **Green Chips for concepts you mastered** and **Red Chips for concepts you missed**.
4. Draws an interactive **Visual Mindmap** showing exactly which topics to revise next.
5. **Runs 100% locally on your computer with zero internet required** — keeping your study notes completely private.

---

## 👥 2. Who is Maevein Tutor For?

### 🎓 1. Students & Exam Candidates
- **For**: School students, college undergraduates, medical/engineering students, and competitive exam test-takers.
- **Why They Love It**: They get instant feedback on open-ended study questions without waiting days for a teacher to grade their paper, and they know exactly what to study to pass their exams.

### 👩‍🏫 2. Teachers & Professors
- **For**: Educators, school teachers, and university lecturers.
- **Why They Love It**: It saves them 10 to 15 hours every week spent manually writing exam rubrics and grading essays. Teachers can upload their lecture slides, generate instant practice quizzes, and let students test themselves in the classroom over the local Wi-Fi router.

### 🏫 3. Schools & Educational Institutions
- **For**: Primary schools, high schools, universities, and coaching institutes with strict data privacy laws (FERPA / GDPR) or poor internet connectivity.
- **Why They Love It**: It runs 100% on the school's local computers. Zero student names or test results leave the school building.

### 📖 4. Self-Learners & Professional Upskillers
- **For**: Anyone learning programming, machine learning, biology, history, or business from online PDFs and ebooks.
- **Why They Love It**: It turns dry reading material into interactive self-assessments that keep them accountable.
