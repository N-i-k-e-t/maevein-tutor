# Maevein Tutor — Official Brand & Design System Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
Maevein Tutor embodies intelligent, empowering, privacy-first AI education. The visual identity combines deep slate tones, electric indigo gradients, and vibrant emerald accents to evoke clarity, trust, and technological innovation.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Slate Backdrop**: `#0f172a` (`hsl(222, 47%, 11%)`) — Main background & dark cards
- **Electric Indigo**: `#4f46e5` (`hsl(243, 75%, 59%)`) — Primary action buttons & brand accents
- **Violet Glow**: `#7c3aed` (`hsl(262, 83%, 58%)`) — AI & Gemma 4 reasoning callouts

### Functional & Feedback Colors
- **Emerald Mastered Green**: `#10b981` / `#ecfdf5` — Mastered concepts & success badges
- **Amber Gold Warning**: `#f59e0b` / `#fffbeb` — Partial mastery & timer warnings
- **Rose Red Gap**: `#f43f5e` / `#fff1f2` — Missed concepts & priority review alerts

---

## 3. Typography Scale
- **Display Headings**: `Outfit`, 800 ExtraBold, sans-serif
- **Section Headings**: `Outfit` / `Inter`, 700 Bold, sans-serif
- **Body & Controls**: `Inter`, 400 Regular / 500 Medium, sans-serif
- **Code & Timers**: `JetBrains Mono` / Monospace, 600 SemiBold

---

## 4. UI Components & Micro-Interactions
- **Glassmorphism Cards**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8); border-radius: 16px; box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.05);`
- **Button Hover State**: `transform: translateY(-2px); box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.3); transition: all 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1);`
