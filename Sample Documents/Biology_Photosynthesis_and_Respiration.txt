BIOLOGY STUDY GUIDE: PHOTOSYNTHESIS AND CELLULAR RESPIRATION
Course Code: BIO-101 | Principles of Modern Biology

1. PHOTOSYNTHESIS OVERVIEW
Photosynthesis is the metabolic process by which photoautotrophic organisms (plants, algae, and cyanobacteria) convert solar light energy into chemical potential energy stored in organic carbohydrates (glucose).

Chemical Reaction Formula:
6 CO2 + 6 H2O + Light Energy -> C6H12O6 + 6 O2

Organelle Site:
Photosynthesis takes place inside chloroplasts, specialized double-membrane organelles containing thylakoids (stacked into grana) suspended within a fluid matrix called the stroma. Chlorophyll a and b pigments absorb photons primarily in the blue and red wavelengths while reflecting green light.

2. TWO MAIN STAGES OF PHOTOSYNTHESIS
A. Light-Dependent Reactions (Occur in Thylakoid Membranes):
- Photons excite electrons in Photosystem II (PSII) and Photosystem I (PSI).
- Photolysis splits water molecules into protons, electrons, and oxygen gas (O2 byproduct).
- Electron Transport Chain (ETC) generates a proton gradient across the thylakoid membrane, powering ATP Synthase to convert ADP into ATP.
- NADP+ reductase reduces NADP+ into NADPH.

B. Light-Independent Reactions / Calvin Cycle (Occur in Chloroplast Stroma):
- Phase 1: Carbon Fixation — Enzyme RuBisCO (Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase-oxygenase) attaches atmospheric CO2 to RuBP, forming 3-PGA.
- Phase 2: Reduction — ATP and NADPH phosphorylate and reduce 3-PGA into Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P).
- Phase 3: Regeneration of RuBP — G3P molecules are reassembled into RuBP to continue the cycle, while remaining G3P exits to form glucose.

3. CELLULAR RESPIRATION OVERVIEW
Cellular respiration is the catabolic pathway that breaks down glucose to produce ATP energy in aerobic organisms.

Chemical Reaction Formula:
C6H12O6 + 6 O2 -> 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + ~36-38 ATP

4. THREE STAGES OF AEROBIC CELL RESPIRATION
- Glycolysis (Cytosol): Glucose (6-carbon) is converted into 2 Pyruvate (3-carbon), yielding net 2 ATP and 2 NADH. Does not require oxygen.
- Krebs Cycle / Citric Acid Cycle (Mitochondrial Matrix): Pyruvate is converted to Acetyl-CoA, producing CO2, 2 ATP, 6 NADH, and 2 FADH2 per glucose.
- Oxidative Phosphorylation (Inner Mitochondrial Membrane): NADH and FADH2 donate electrons to the ETC. Oxygen acts as the final electron acceptor, combining with protons to form H2O. ATP Synthase generates ~32-34 ATP molecules via chemiosmosis.
