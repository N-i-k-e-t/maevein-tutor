## 💡 How to Test in Maevein Tutor App

1. Open **Maevein Tutor** in your browser (`http://localhost:3000`).
2. Click **"Get Started"** to enter the **Upload Study Material** view.
3. Either:
   - Select one of the built-in pre-loaded cards (*Machine Learning*, *Biology*, *CS Algorithms*), or
   - Drag and drop any `.txt` / `.pdf` file from this `Sample Documents/` folder into the dropzone.
4. Click **"Generate Questions with Gemma 4"** to trigger document concept extraction and Bloom's Taxonomy question synthesis.
5. Attempt the test to experience **Dual-Call Evaluation**, **Green/Red Source Concept Chips**, and the **Interactive Knowledge Mindmap Graph**!
